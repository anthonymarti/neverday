# Business brief — concept and launch gates

## Decision

Neverday is a proposed branded retailer of finite data-only travel eSIM packs, initially for U.S.-based independent travelers with a 7–15 day trip to Japan. It would not own network infrastructure. Wider demo destinations are examples, not launch coverage.

Positioning: **Go a little further.** Compatibility before purchase; finite price/quota; guided installation; a visible balance/expiry; manual top-ups; no automatic renewal. No domestic voice, SMS, numbers, porting or emergency-calling service is proposed.

Domestic cellular/MVNO service adds carrier/MVNE agreements, numbers, porting, emergency-service and operational/regulatory responsibilities. Recurring global data adds cross-country cost/FUP/roaming exposure before demand is proven. Finite travel packs are the focused concept to validate; feasibility depends on actual terms.

## Offering and sample prices

Japan 3 GB/7 days $9, 5 GB/15 days $14, 10 GB/30 days $23. These are UX sample prices, NOT wholesale quotes, guaranteed retail offers or validated margins. Top-up 3 GB/$7 is likewise illustrative.

Revenue would come from retail margin on contracted finite packs and eligible top-ups. Provider selection is deliberately not declared until rates, funding, coverage, restrictions and refunds are verified.

## Zero-upfront launch

No verified arrangement currently lets Neverday immediately provision branded service without cash reserves. A no-setup/no-monthly fee API can still require a prepaid wallet. Payment success does not establish withdrawable funds. Initial/normal payout delays, holds, disputes and non-returned processor fees matter.

Closest possible alternatives:
1. Obtain actual supplier credit whose term covers real settlement availability, with no deposit/minimums; unverified.
2. An approved affiliate program leaves the provider as seller/payment collector; it may avoid Neverday inventory financing but is a different launch.
3. Fund a small supplier wallet plus refund/dispute reserve. Actual unavoidable amount is unknown pending terms.

Do not accept payment before supplier/processor approval, funded fulfillment, legal/tax classification, permitted hosting and authorized device testing.

## Acquisition

Interview 15 U.S. travelers with booked Japan trips, observe five full demo journeys, publish an original Japan arrival/compatibility checklist, and recruit through personal networks and relevant opt-in communities without spam. Offer concierge help for the first 20 funded pilot trips. Measure conversion, compatibility failure, support time and actual contribution. Founder time is not free economic acquisition.

## Unit economics: assumptions, USD

Retail $14 less assumed wholesale $6, processing $0.71 (illustrative 2.9%+$0.30), support $0.70, refund/dispute allowance $0.42, variable operations $0.28 = $5.89 contribution before acquisition/tax/fixed cost/labor. With $3 acquisition, $2.89 remains.

Wholesale sensitivity: $4 → $7.89 pre-acquisition; $6 → $5.89; $10 → $1.89, or negative $1.11 after $3 acquisition. Tax and fixed costs are excluded, not assumed legally zero.

One immediate order at assumed $6 supplier charge requires at least $6 supplier bridge plus any wallet minimum/deposit and reserve. Ten orders/day during an assumed 14-day initial funds gap requires $840 supplier bridge before taxes, FX, fees, refunds/disputes, deposits or held funds. The 14 days is a scenario, not a promised processor payout.

## First-subscriber gates

- Written supplier KYC/entity/jurisdiction approval; SKU rates; setup/minimum/deposit/prepay/credit terms; country/network/speed/FUP/hotspot/routing; activation and refund rules.
- Processor merchant eligibility, settlement/reserves, refunds/dispute fees, FX and tax treatment.
- Actual legal entity/jurisdiction choice and review. U.S. customer focus does not establish the owner's jurisdiction or regulatory exemption. Determine telecom reseller classification, applicable FCC/USAC/state obligations, tax/nexus, privacy, sanctions/KYC and consumer disclosures. Verify destination roaming restrictions.
- Secure provider/payment integrations and funded immediate fulfillment/refund capacity.
- Real authorized service tests, outage/support procedures and accessibility.
- Hosting permitted for intended business use within authorized budget.
